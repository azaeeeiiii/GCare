1. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run GCare:

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored in protected form).
  • Reports you submit: descriptions, photos, time and location of the incident.
  • Location: your approximate or precise location, only if you allow it, to show nearby alerts and place reports on the map.
  • Technical data: basic device and browser information used to keep the site working and secure.

2. How we use your information

  • Create and secure your account.
  • Show alerts, reports and safety information that are relevant to your area.
  • Let you report incidents and join volunteer activities.
  • Verify reports and prevent spam or abuse.
  • Improve GCare and fix problems.

3. What other people can see

Incident reports may appear on the community feed and safety map. Your email address and password are never shown to other users.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share report details with local authorities or response teams when it helps people in need, or when the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as the law requires. Reports that matter for safety records may be kept after your account is deleted, with personal details removed where possible.

6. Security

We use reasonable organizational and technical safeguards to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep it private.

7. Your rights

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) you may, among other rights:

  • ask to see the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • object to certain uses of your data, or ask us to delete it;
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

To use these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children

GCare is not meant for children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Cookies and local storage

GCare may store small pieces of data in your browser, such as keeping you logged in or remembering preferences. You can clear this at any time from your browser settings.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

11. Contact us

Privacy questions or requests? Reach us through the Contact page.

Also read our Terms of Use, or get in touch through the Contact page.
